# Shipping and Delivery Policy - ApexTech Demo Store

1. Shipping Options and Rates
- Standard Ground Shipping: 3-5 business days. Free for orders over $50; flat rate $4.99 for orders under $50.
- Expedited Priority Shipping: 2 business days. Flat rate $12.99.
- Overnight Express Shipping: 1 business day (order placed by 1:00 PM EST). Flat rate $24.99.
- International Shipping: 7-14 business days to over 45 countries. Calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.

2. Order Processing Schedule
Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding major federal holidays. Orders received by 2:00 PM EST typically ship the same day. Orders placed over the weekend ship the following Monday.

3. Package Tracking
Real-time tracking is provided for all shipments via FedEx, UPS, and USPS. You will receive an SMS and email notification with tracking numbers upon carrier pickup.

4. Lost, Stolen, or Delayed Packages
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but you cannot locate the package, please check with neighbors and building management, then wait 24 hours as carriers occasionally scan packages prematurely.
- If a package remains unlocated 48 hours after marked delivery, contact support or notify this bot to open a formal carrier lost package investigation and dispatch a replacement.

5. Address Corrections
Address corrections can be made free of charge within 2 hours of order placement. Once handed over to the courier, rerouting fees of $15 may be charged by the carrier.
