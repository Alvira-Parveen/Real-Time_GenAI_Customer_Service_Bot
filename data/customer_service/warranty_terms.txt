# Extended Warranty and Protection Plan - ApexCare Protection

1. ApexCare Coverage Overview
ApexCare is our premium accidental damage protection and extended warranty service available for all ApexTech devices. It extends the standard 1-year warranty to 3 full years of comprehensive coverage.

2. Accidental Damage Coverage
ApexCare covers:
- Cracked screens and liquid spills
- Accidental drops and structural housing cracks
- Power surge failures and battery degradation below 80% original capacity
- Unlimited claims per year with a nominal $29 deductible for repairs or $49 for complete unit replacement.

3. Filing an ApexCare Claim
Claims can be initiated directly through this chatbot by saying "file warranty claim" or by visiting warranty.apextech-demo.com. A prepaid overnight shipping box will be dispatched within 24 hours.

4. Transferability and Cancellation
ApexCare can be transferred to a new owner if you sell your device, and can be cancelled within 60 days of purchase for a 100% prorated refund.
