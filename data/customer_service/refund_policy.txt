# Return and Refund Policy - ApexTech Demo Store

1. Return Window
Customers may return eligible items within 30 calendar days of delivery date for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in original condition, including packaging, manuals, accessories, and documentation.

2. Non-Returnable Items
The following products are strictly non-returnable:
- Downloadable software licenses and digital gift cards
- Consumable items (thermal paste, cleaning kits) once unsealed
- Custom-built or personalized computer configurations
- Products marked "Final Sale" or "As-Is Clearance"

3. Return Shipping Costs
- Defective or Damaged Products: ApexTech provides a prepaid return shipping label.
- Buyer's Remorse or Change of Mind: Return shipping fee of $7.99 will be deducted from the total refund amount.

4. Refund Processing Time
Once our return inspection center receives your item, allow 3 to 5 business days for quality inspection. After approval:
- Credit/Debit Cards: 3-7 business days depending on your banking institution.
- PayPal / Digital Wallets: 24 to 48 hours.
- Store Credit / Gift Cards: Instant credited upon approval.

5. Restocking Fees
A 15% restocking fee applies only to opened electronics returned without original packaging or missing secondary accessories.

6. Damaged or Defective Items upon Delivery
If your order arrives damaged, broken, or malfunctioning, please take clear photos and upload them to our customer service chatbot within 48 hours of delivery for immediate replacement or expedited refund.
